Who We Are

Refined design.
Radical purpose.

Universal Water Alliance was born from a simple conviction: luxury and social good are not opposites. We craft exceptional water bottles — handfinished, sustainably sourced, built to last a lifetime — and use every sale to fund clean water access in communities that have none.

We don't just donate bottles. We build wells, transport infrastructure, and train local teams to maintain water systems independently for generations.

"Clean water is not a charity. It is a foundation upon which everything else — health, education, opportunity — is built."

From Sub-Saharan Africa to rural South Asia — we go where the need is greatest, building permanent infrastructure that communities own and maintain.

What We Do

Six ways we
move water.

🏗️
Infrastructure

Well Construction

We fund, design, and build freshwater wells in villages where women and children walk 5+ miles daily to collect water. Each well serves up to 500 people for 20+ years.

🚛
Direct Aid

Bottle Distribution

For every UWA bottle sold, we donate a premium filtration bottle to a family in need — allowing them to safely carry and store the water they collect.

📡
Technology

Water Monitoring

We deploy IoT sensor networks to monitor water quality and flow in real time. Communities receive alerts and we dispatch response teams within 48 hours.

🌱
Community

Education Programs

Hygiene and water safety training for 10,000+ community members annually, delivered by local ambassadors trained and certified by Universal Water Alliance.

🤝
Partnership

Corporate Gifting

Luxury branded UWA sets for your team or clients — with each order triggering a matched donation. Impact reporting included for every corporate partner.

📊
Accountability

Transparent Reporting

Every donor receives a quarterly impact report with GPS coordinates, photos, and water quality data from the projects their purchase funded.
